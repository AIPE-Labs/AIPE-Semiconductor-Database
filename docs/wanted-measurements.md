# Wanted measurements

Generated by `python scripts/generate_wanted.py` from the canonical corpus; do not edit by hand.

The database holds 163 devices, all from manufacturer simulation models. It holds no laboratory measurement. Any measurement of any listed device is therefore new information; the table shows where even a model value is absent.

| Measurement | Protocol category | Devices with no data at all | Devices with no measurement |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Capacitances and output-capacitance energy (Ciss, Coss, Crss, Eoss against voltage) | `capacitance` | 163 of 163 | 163 of 163 |
| Gate charge | `gate_charge` | 163 of 163 | 163 of 163 |
| Static conduction (on-state voltage drop or RDS(on) against current and temperature) | `static_conduction` | 0 of 163 | 163 of 163 |
| Reverse (third-quadrant) conduction and reverse recovery | `reverse_conduction` | 0 of 163 | 163 of 163 |
| Double-pulse switching energies (Eon, Eoff against current, voltage, temperature, gate resistance) | `dpt` | 0 of 163 | 163 of 163 |
| Thermal impedance junction to case | `thermal` | 0 of 163 | 163 of 163 |

How to read it:

- **No data at all**: not even the manufacturer model gives this quantity. A measurement, or a manufacturer's own data under an open licence, fills a hole.
- **No measurement**: a model value exists, but nothing measured confirms it. Measurements at the model's own grid points make the two directly comparable.
- Data for devices that are not in the database yet are welcome too.

Limits of this list: it counts presence, not quality, and it does not rank devices against each other. For a specific converter design, `aipe-devices measurement-requests` produces a prioritised request ([docs/dab-loss-screening.md](dab-loss-screening.md)).
